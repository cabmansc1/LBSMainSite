# Print services

Working documents for the print side of the business — business cards, flyers,
unmailed postcards, magnets and the rest. **Nothing here is part of the live
site.**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `LBS-print-rate-sheet.xlsx` | Price calculator. Enter wholesale cost, get a quotable price. |
| `proof-and-revision-terms.md` | Terms to paste into quotes. Short version for routine jobs, full version for larger ones. |

## Using the rate sheet

1. **Setup tab** — set your target margin, rounding, minimum order value, rush
   uplift and design hourly rate. Everything else follows from these.
2. **Rate Sheet tab** — enter your wholesale cost per order in column D only.
   Prices, margins and per-unit figures calculate themselves. A blank cost
   leaves the row blank rather than showing a wrong number.
3. **Design Fees tab** — flat and hourly rates for work that is genuinely new
   artwork rather than reusing a design you already made.
4. **Quote Builder tab** — type a product and quantity, add a design fee, mark
   it rush or not, and read the total.

Yellow cells with blue text are the ones to edit. Everything else calculates.

Only `D5` is filled in, as an example of the expected format — it is a
placeholder wholesale cost for 250 business cards, not a real quote. Replace it.

The starting values on the Setup and Design Fees tabs are chosen to be
plausible, not researched. They are a starting point to argue with once real
jobs have been run.

## Note on the example

At the placeholder numbers, 250 business cards comes out *below* the minimum
order value and the sheet says so. That is the calculator working, not a bug —
small reorders are exactly the jobs that cost more attention than they earn, and
the right answer is usually to roll them into the customer's next mailing
invoice.

## Google Business Profile

`google-business-profile.md` is the setup pack for the profile — the
decisions, the categories, and paste-ready copy. It exists because for
local print searches the map pack outranks the website, so the profile
matters more than any page.

It is the one item here that cannot be done from this repo: editing a
Business Profile needs a signed-in Google account and verified
ownership. The pack takes it as far as it can go without that.

Two things in it are load-bearing and easy to get wrong — the PO Box
address will be rejected, and a second profile for printing risks
suspending the one that already exists.
